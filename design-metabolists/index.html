    About

    Design Metabolists is a Singapore practice of chartered architects, professional designers and development consultants, established in 1973.

    We provide architectural design and consultancy for residential, corporate, government and civic clients, working under a quality management system from first sketch to handover. Designing to tackle the small details for your big plans.

    Services

    • New buildings from concept to completion.

    • Feasibility and planning advice for developers and owners.

    • Precinct and estate improvement works for public housing.

    • Extending and adapting existing buildings.

    • Early-stage studies and large-site plans.

    • Approvals, tender and site supervision.
