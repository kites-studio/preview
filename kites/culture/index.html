Let’s talk

Hello. We’re Made by Kites.

A creative house.
A practical kind of magic.

We bring design and technology together to make things that feel good to use. Websites, products, experiences — and interesting ideas that don’t fit neatly in a box.

Personality matters.

A business has its own character. Its website should have one too. We look for the details that make it yours, then give them room to breathe.

Purpose comes first.

A lovely interface still needs to help someone. Find an answer. Make a choice. Start a conversation. We design for those little moments.

How we think

Clear, before clever.

People should understand where they are and what comes next. A little surprise is welcome when the basics feel effortless.

Calm, with a pulse.

Motion that responds. Type with room to breathe. Personality in the right places. Thoughtful experiences don’t have to feel still.

Make. Learn. Refine.

Ideas get better when you can try them. We value working versions, honest conversations and the small improvements that add up.
